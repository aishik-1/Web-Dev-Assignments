Scroll Inspection Block 1

position: sticky toggles between relative and fixed positioning based on the user's scroll offset relative to its containing block. It requires at least one threshold (such as top: 0) to be declared.

Scroll Inspection Block 2

Card overlay elements make use of position: absolute calculated relative to their closest positioned ancestor (in our case, the parent .project-card with position: relative).
